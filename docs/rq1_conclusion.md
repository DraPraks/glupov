# RQ1 conclusion

Across the 10 rated study clips, no VBench dimension was significantly associated with viewer enjoyment after correcting for multiple tests.

Aesthetic quality (Kendall’s τ-b = +0.27) and imaging quality (+0.22) had the largest positive estimates, but all six estimable associations had wide 95% intervals that included zero. The study did not establish a reliable link between the scores and enjoyment, and it does not rule one out.

See the [findings](rq1_findings.md) for the estimates and the checks that could not be calculated.
