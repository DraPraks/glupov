# RQ1 conclusion

After Holm correction at 0.05, none of the scored VBench dimensions associate with mean enjoyment. The family is the seven columns marked `scored` in `data/dimension_applicability.csv`. Six of them have a defined Kendall tau-b on the 10 clips that have survey ratings. Every one of those six has Holm p = 1.0 and `reject_holm` False in `rq1_outputs/rq1_kendall_results.csv`. Their 95% bootstrap intervals all include both negative and positive values. Aesthetic quality has the largest tau-b (0.26967994498529685) and imaging quality the next (0.22473328748774737). Subject consistency, background consistency, motion smoothness, and human anatomy are closer to zero, with mixed signs. Failure to reject is not evidence that an association is absent. Ten clips leave wide intervals.

Dynamic degree does not supply a coefficient. It is 1.0 on every scored clip, so tau-b is undefined. The results file leaves that row’s tau-b, interval, and p-values blank (`analysis_status` `no_variation`) and does not reject it. Median-enjoyment tau-b is also undefined: every eligible clip has median enjoyment 3, and `kendall_tau_b_median` is blank on every row. That check is not a second confirmatory test. Like and dislike text was not part of the coefficient. Spearman was not estimated.

`source_id` is still a placeholder (`drama_01`–`drama_11`). The survey has no Clip 11 enjoyment column, so `drama_clip_11` is outside the association. The notebook wrote the coefficients because the placeholder ids are unique, not because they are verified drama ids. These are pilot associations, not confirmatory claims about independent dramas.
