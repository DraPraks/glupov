# RQ1 conclusion

Across the 10 rated clips, none of the six tested VBench or VBench-2.0 dimensions reached statistical significance after Holm correction at $\alpha=0.05$.

Aesthetic quality ($\tau_b=+0.27$) and imaging quality ($\tau_b=+0.22$) had the largest positive estimates. All six 95% intervals were wide and included zero, leaving the direction and strength of the associations uncertain.
